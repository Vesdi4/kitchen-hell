Kitchen Hell Guide

Everything the game doesn't tell you. Tables on this page are read straight from the game's data, so the numbers are always current.

The basics

Click the big steak to earn steaks, the game's currency. Spend them on kitchen equipment (the shop on the right). Equipment cooks steaks for you every second, even while the game is closed (at half speed for up to 2 hours, unless you buy Chef's Secrets that extend it).

  • Steaks per second is the number under your total. Clicks add on top.
  • Every piece of equipment costs 14% more than the last one of the same kind.
  • Upgrades show up above the shop when you can almost afford them. Most double one building or your clicks. They're usually the best purchase on screen.
  • Your career moves through 11 stages based on lifetime steaks. Each stage changes the steak, the photo and unlocks something new.
  • Tabs stay hidden until they're useful. A pulsing dot on a tab means it just opened.
Early tip: Click a lot for the first few minutes, buy every cheap upgrade, then keep the cheapest building with the best value. Equipment soon makes far more than clicking.

Career stages

You move up a stage when your lifetime steaks reach the target. Stages are never lost, not even after a Cook-off.

Kitchen equipment

Golden dumplings

Every 2 to 6 minutes a golden dumpling floats onto the screen for about 12 seconds. Click it before it disappears. It's the single biggest boost in the game.

Chef Skills

Bought with steaks, up to level . They reset after a Cook-off (Family Notebook keeps a quarter).

Service styles

Pick one focus for the run in the Chef Skills tab. You can switch every 5 minutes.

Which one? Fast Food if you click a lot, Slow Cooking if you leave the game open idle, Street Food if you hunt dumplings.

Reputation & Hospitality

Every achievement gives +2% Reputation (the Rep chip at the top). Reputation does nothing alone. Hospitality upgrades (Clean Towels, Soft Music, Candlelit Tables…) turn it into steaks per second, each one adding a share of your Reputation. More achievements make every Hospitality upgrade stronger, so collecting them pays off.

Shadow achievements (secret, weird challenges) don't count toward Reputation. They're there for bragging rights.

Cook-off & Stars

The Cook-off is the prestige reset. You give up your steaks, equipment, upgrades and Chef Skills for the run, and earn Stars.

  • Stars = cube root of (lifetime steaks ÷ 1,000,000). So 1 trillion lifetime steaks is 100 Stars total. You only get the new ones.
  • Every Star gives +3% to everything, forever. Past 1,000 Stars each extra Star counts for less.
  • Stars are also spent on Chef's Secrets (below). Spending them does not remove their +3% bonus.
  • Kept forever: stages, achievements, Stars, Chef's Secrets, Sourdough levels, seasonal dishes, Signatures.
When to Cook-off? The first time, at around 10 Stars. After that, when the pending Stars would roughly double what you have. A fresh run with more Stars catches up fast.

Chef's Secrets

The permanent upgrade tree, bought with Stars in the Cook-off tab. Start with Kitchen Pass, it opens everything else.

Good first picks: Kitchen Pass, Sous-Chef (auto-clicks), Packed Lunch, Lucky Chopsticks, Coupon Book, then Recipe Box I to keep your best upgrade between runs.

Sourdough

After 1 billion lifetime steaks you get one Sourdough loaf every 2 real hours (offline too, up to 12 waiting) and sometimes from golden dumplings. Spend loaves in the shop to level up a building: +1% output per level, forever. A level costs its number in loaves (level 5 costs 5). Spread levels on your strongest building.

Side systems

Each one has its own tab once unlocked. None of them is required, but each one adds a lot once you learn it.

Kitchen Demon

Unlocks at the Prep Cook stage.

A little demon in your oven. Feed it steaks or hit equipment milestones to level it up. Higher levels unlock Pacts: auras you equip for a permanent bonus. The demon also makes short timed Demands ("buy 10 of X in 60 seconds"). Complete them for a reward.

Keep it fed. Equip the Pact that matches how you play (clicking or idle).

Seasons

Unlocks at the Knife Guy stage.

Real-calendar events: New Year Banquet (January), Spring Market (March to April), Summer BBQ (June to August), Hell Night (October), Feast of Lights (December). Each gives a passive bonus while it lasts, and seasonal dishes drop from golden dumplings and cooking. Dishes are kept forever (+1% steaks per second or +2% cooking each). The last dish of every season only drops from cooking.

The Menu Swap Pass upgrade lets a run pick any season, so you can collect them all year.

Pests & Critics

Unlocks at 200M lifetime steaks or the Recipe Blogger stage.

Cockroaches scurry over your steak. Each one steals 5% of production while it's there. Click one several times to squash it: it pays back everything it stole with interest (+15%, or +30% for golden ones). Sometimes a food critic challenges you to a timed duel.

Letting roaches build up for a while and then squashing them all gives a bigger payout than squashing each one right away.

Hellfire Contract

Unlocks with 10 Line Cooks at the Recipe Blogger stage.

A ladder of infernal clauses you sign with steaks. Each clause makes Line Cooks and the whole kitchen stronger, but raises Damnation (0 to 3). Higher Damnation means golden dumplings sometimes come out Charred, with a risky outcome. Exorcism pauses Damnation for 30 real minutes. The Truce ends it for the run, at a price.

Sign the first clauses early. They're cheap and Damnation stays low.

Herb Garden

Unlocks with a Grill Station at the Head of Accounting stage.

Plant herbs that grow in real time (one tick per minute, also while closed). Ripe plants give small passive bonuses. Harvesting pays steaks or a buff. An empty plot next to two ripe parents can sprout a crossbreed, which is how you discover rarer plants.

Leave gaps between ripe plants to get crossbreeds. Harvest just before a plant dies.

Ingredient Exchange

Unlocks when you own a Walk-in Freezer.

A trading minigame. Ingredients from Rice Sacks to Black Truffles have prices that drift up and down. Buy low, sell high. Prices are measured in seconds of your production, so the game stays useful at any size.

Every ingredient swings around its normal price. Buy when it's far below and wait.

Hot Sauce Rack

Unlocks when you own a Hot Sauce Lab.

Splash sauces into a few rack slots. Each lasts 10 real minutes and gives a bonus (clicks, production, dumpling rate, cheaper equipment…). Some pairs make a combo. Every splash builds spice tolerance, which weakens all effects, and too much triggers a Meltdown.

Don't splash nonstop. Let tolerance cool down and use sauces before a big dumpling combo.

Shrine (Saints of the Line)

Unlocks when you own a Diner.

Three niches, Gold, Copper and Tin, hold saints at 100%, 50% and 25% strength. Each saint has a blessing and a curse, and both scale with the niche. Moving saints costs Devotion (max 3, +1 per real hour). Saints stay through Cook-offs, and each Cook-off gives one free placement.

Put the saint whose curse you don't care about in Gold.

Cursed Cookbook

Unlocks when you own a Molecular Kitchen.

Cast recipe-spells with Ink, which refills over real time (slower when the book is nearly dry). Spells range from instant steaks to summoning a golden dumpling or making your equipment haunted. Every spell can misfire, and it's more likely when the book is dry or you cast too often.

Keep the book mostly full. Golden Dumpling Invocation during a Dinner Rush is the classic combo.

Hell Shifts

Unlocks at the Head Chef stage, or after your first Cook-off.

Handicapped runs (Power Outage, Health Inspection, Burnt Hand, Broken Supplier, Dumpling Drought, Gas Leak). Start one with a Cook-off and reach the target to earn a permanent +4% bonus. The Simmer switch is also here.

Do them once you have a solid set of Chef's Secrets. They're much easier with Stars behind you.

Endgame: Signatures & Inferno ranks

Reach the last stage, Kitchen Overlord, to open the endless phase.

FAQ

Where is my save?
In your browser, on this site. It saves automatically. Use Options → Export to back it up or move it to another device. Clearing browser data deletes it.
Does the game run while closed?
Yes. Equipment keeps cooking at 50% for up to 2 hours. Overnight Proofing and Closed for Holidays (Chef's Secrets) extend that.
I'm stuck. Nothing is affordable.
That's usually the moment for a Cook-off. If you can't yet, buy the cheapest upgrade and wait for a golden dumpling.
Can I buy more than one at a time?
Yes. Switch the shop to ×10 or ×100. The building name then shows how many you're buying, and you can sell the same way.
