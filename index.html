How to cook

  • Click the big steak to earn steaks. Golden Dumplings that float by give big boosts: click them!
  • Equipment in the store cooks steaks every second. Each one costs 14% more than the last.
  • Upgrades show up on the rack above the equipment as you progress. Hover one to read its ticket.
  • Grab Golden Dumplings when they appear. They start Dinner Rushes and fill the Tip Jar.
  • Train Chef Skills, unlock achievements for Reputation, and enter Cook-offs for permanent Stars.
  • Become the Kitchen Overlord to finish the main journey and unlock endless Signature Dishes.
